# Lead Magnet

## What it is

A free asset that solves one real problem and, in solving it, demonstrates
you can solve the bigger one. It exists to trade genuine value for an email
address and permission to follow up.

## Build this one

> **"The 12 Questions That Decide Your CMMC Scope"**
> A short guide plus a worksheet that helps a contractor work out what is
> actually in scope before they spend a dollar on compliance.

Extracted from project 03. Two to four pages.

## Why this one rather than a checklist

- **It addresses the expensive mistake.** Scoping too wide costs real money,
  and most readers do not know they are about to make it.
- **It is genuinely useful alone.** Someone can act on it without hiring you.
  That is what makes them trust you.
- **It self-qualifies.** Anyone who completes it has told themselves they have
  a scoping problem — and scoping is the small first engagement you sell.
- **It is hard to copy.** A "CMMC checklist" is a commodity. Scoping judgement
  is not.

## Structure

1. **Page 1 — Why scope decides your cost.** Every asset in scope must meet
   110 requirements. Wide scope is the difference between a $6k and a $40k
   project.
2. **Page 2 — The 12 questions.** From `scoping-interview.md`, including at
   least three shadow-path questions.
3. **Page 3 — The five asset categories**, with the distinction between
   contractor-risk-managed and out-of-scope made concrete.
4. **Page 4 — What to do next.** Three options: do it yourself with this,
   book a scoping session, or read the deeper guide. All three are fine.

## Rules

- **Give away the real thing.** A gated worksheet with the useful parts removed
  destroys trust faster than no lead magnet at all.
- **No email wall on page 1.** Let them see value before you ask.
- **One CTA**, repeated. Not four.
- **Put your name and face on it.** People hire people.

## The follow-up sequence

Five emails over two weeks. Value first, offer last.

| # | Day | Subject | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Your scoping worksheet | Deliver it. One line about what to do first. |
| 2 | 2 | The most expensive CMMC mistake | Scoping too wide. A concrete before/after. |
| 3 | 5 | "We'll just put everything in scope" | Why that instinct costs three times as much. |
| 4 | 9 | What an assessor actually asks for | Evidence, not documents. Builds authority. |
| 5 | 14 | Want me to scope it with you? | The offer. One clear CTA. |

Then a monthly newsletter. Do not let the list go cold — it is the only
marketing asset you own outright.

## Where to put it

- A dedicated page on your site with no navigation and one form
- The featured link on your LinkedIn profile
- The close of every article and every talk
- The PS line of cold outreach

## Measuring it

| Metric | Healthy |
|---|---|
| Landing page → email | 20–35% |
| Email 5 → booked call | 2–5% |
| Booked call → paid scoping | 30–50% |

Track these in the dashboard on your profile page. If page-to-email is under
15%, the promise is wrong. If call-to-paid is under 20%, you are attracting
people with no contract requiring compliance — tighten the targeting, not the
pitch.
