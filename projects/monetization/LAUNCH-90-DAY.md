# 90-Day Launch Plan

Assumes evenings and weekends alongside a full-time job — roughly 10–15 hours a
week. Adjust the dates, keep the order.

## Days 1–30 — Build the asset

**Goal:** one finished product and enough credibility to be taken seriously.

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read 800-171 Rev 2 and 800-171A properly. Build the lab (project 07). |
| 2 | Rewrite all 110 implementation statements in your own voice (project 01). |
| 3 | Assess your own lab end to end. Produce a complete worked SSP and POA&M. |
| 4 | Convert the kit to .docx/.xlsx. Build the lead magnet. Rewrite LinkedIn. |

**End of month 1 you have:** a documentation kit, a worked example from a real
environment, a lead magnet, and a profile that says what you do.

**Do not skip week 3.** Filling in your own template against a real environment
is what turns theory into something you can defend on a call.

## Days 31–60 — Get in front of people

**Goal:** first conversations, first sale of any size.

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| 5 | Publish the lead magnet. Post twice on LinkedIn. Build a list of 100 local targets from SAM.gov. |
| 6 | 25 cold emails. 20 LinkedIn connections. Two posts. |
| 7 | 25 more emails, plus follow-ups. Two posts. Approach two MSPs about referrals. |
| 8 | Launch the kit on Gumroad. Tell the email list. Two posts. |

**Realistic outcome:** 3–8 conversations, 1–3 product sales, possibly one
scoping session. If you get zero replies from 50 emails, the problem is
targeting — you are emailing companies with no CMMC obligation.

## Days 61–90 — Convert and systematise

**Goal:** first service engagement, and a repeatable process.

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| 9 | Run any scoping sessions booked. Quote the follow-on work. |
| 10 | Deliver. Track actual hours against your estimate. |
| 11 | Write the first engagement up as an anonymised case study. Ask for a referral. |
| 12 | Recalibrate the scope calculator with real hours. Raise prices. Plan the next 90 days. |

**Realistic outcome by day 90:** 1–2 paid service engagements, $2,000–$8,000
total revenue, one case study, one testimonial, and a pipeline.

## What "on track" actually looks like

| Day | Reasonable |
|---|---|
| 30 | Kit built, lab working, worked SSP done |
| 60 | 5+ conversations, 1+ sale of any size |
| 90 | 1–2 paid engagements, one case study |

If day 90 arrives with no revenue but real conversations, you are not failing —
compliance sales cycles run 30–90 days and a Q1 conversation often closes in
Q2. If day 90 arrives with no *conversations*, stop building and fix targeting.

## The failure mode to watch for

**Overlearning, under-shipping.** Reading a fourth NIST publication feels like
progress and is not. After week 2, no new studying unless a live client
question demands it.

The other one: building the perfect product before talking to anyone. Ship the
kit at 80% and let the first buyer tell you what is missing.
